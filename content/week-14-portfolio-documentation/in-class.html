Our last session is online, on Zoom, at the usual time. The link is in the syllabus and in the Canvas announcement. Join from a laptop, with your portfolio page open in a browser tab and your camera on.

Part 1: Portfolio Reviews (about 10 minutes each)

  • Share your screen and walk us through your portfolio page from the top, as a hiring manager would read it.
  • The rest of the class watches for one thing: could someone who was not in the room understand the product, the hero experience and what testing changed, in two minutes?
  • Each person gets two or three specific fixes. Make them in the following days; the page is yours to keep improving after the term.

Part 2: Course Feedback (30 min)

This course is redesigned every year from what students say at the end of the term. We use a shared online board in place of the whiteboard. For each week, or each block of weeks, add notes on:

  • What worked and should stay.
  • What was too fast, too slow, too late or too early.
  • What you wish you had known at the start.

Be specific. "Hardware too late" changed the whole second half of this course; "it was fine" changes nothing.

Part 3: Close (10 min)

What happens next with your projects, the class library, and how to stay in touch.
