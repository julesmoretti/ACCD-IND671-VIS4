Part 2: Test Your Interface

You have been building your own project interface since Week 2, and in Week 3 you wrote out its requirements with priorities. Now you find out whether any of it works for someone other than you.

Test the clickable version you built in Week 3. Run a software test following the Week 4 Software Testing Protocol page:

  • 3–4 classmates, one at a time, about fifteen minutes each
  • 3–4 tasks, taken directly from your Week 3 requirements list — prioritise your Must-haves
  • Break each task into steps, score functionality per step, and log emotional reactions per element, using the tables in the protocol

Two rules that matter more than the rest: do not explain how the interface works before they start, and do not defend it while they struggle. Anything you explain is something you can no longer test, and the uncomfortable silence while someone is stuck is where the finding lives.

This is a rehearsal. The same scoring returns in Week 9 for your hardware prototype, run three times and compared — and at that point it is worth 20 points of your final project grade. Learning the instrument here on a small interface is the cheap way to learn it.
