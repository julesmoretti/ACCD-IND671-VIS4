This is the last working class before the final presentations next Friday. Bring your form prototype, your round 2 tables, your R1–R2 comparison, and everything you need to run your demo.

Exercise 1: Round 2 Review (30 min)

In groups, each person shows their comparison table:

  1. Which scores went up, and which change caused it?
  2. Which steps are still scoring 2 or lower? These are your hard problems.
  3. Did anything get worse on the real form that worked on cardboard?

Exercise 2: Pick the Refinements and Plan Round 3 (20 min)

  • Choose the two or three hard problems you will fix before round 3. Not everything: you have days, not weeks. For each, write what you will change and which score you expect to move.
  • Participants for round 3: ArtCenter students outside GradID. They know neither your project nor the discipline. Book them today; round 3 has to be finished by Thursday night.
  • Same tasks, so the scores compare. Focus the session on the steps that still score badly.

Exercise 3: Presentation Outline (20 min)

Open the Final Project & Presentation brief. Write the outline of your presentation section by section, and next to each section, the evidence you will show. Mark anything you do not have yet. Leave a slot for your round 3 results and the finding that surprised you most.

Exercise 4: Demo Dry Run (40 min)

In groups, each person runs their live demo from a cold start: server, React app, Arduino, hero experience end to end. Time it. Note every step that failed or needed a restart. A demo that works only on the third try will fail in front of the room.

Write your start-up steps on one card and keep it with your prototype.

Exercise 5: Build and Desk Crits (rest of class)

Fix what failed in the dry run first, then the refinements for round 3. Desk crits run during this time.
