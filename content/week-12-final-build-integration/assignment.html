Apply the two or three refinements you chose in class. Above all, the hero experience must run end to end from a cold start, every time.

Anything you do not finish on the finish (paint, final materials, photography) is not lost: next week's portfolio assignment is where it gets completed.

Part 3: Compile All Three Rounds

This is the user testing evidence for your final project (20 of 100 points).
