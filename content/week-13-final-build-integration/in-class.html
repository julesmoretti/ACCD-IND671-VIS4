Last working class before the final presentations. Bring your prototype, laptop, and everything needed to run the demo.

Exercise 1: Demo Dry Run (40 min)

In groups of three, each person runs their live demo from a cold start: server, React app, Arduino, hero experience end to end. Time it. Note every step that failed or needed a restart. A demo that works only on the third try will fail in front of the room.

Exercise 2: Presentation Outline (20 min)

Write the outline of your final presentation against the Week 14 brief. Check you have evidence for each section, especially the three-round comparison and the finding that surprised you.

Exercise 3: Build and Desk Crits (rest of class)

Fix what failed in the dry run first, then finish. Desk crits run during this time.
