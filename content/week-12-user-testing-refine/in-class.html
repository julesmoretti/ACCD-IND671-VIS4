Bring your form prototype, your round 2 tables and your R1–R2 comparison.

Exercise 1: Round 2 Review (30 min)

In groups of three, each person shows their comparison table:

  1. Which scores went up, and which change caused it?
  2. Which steps are still scoring 2 or lower? These are your hard problems.
  3. Did anything get worse on the real form that worked on cardboard?

Exercise 2: Pick the Refinements (15 min)

Choose the two or three hard problems you will fix before round 3. Not everything: you have one week. For each, write what you will change and which score you expect to move.

Exercise 3: Plan Round 3 (15 min)

  • Participants: ArtCenter students outside GradID. They know neither your project nor the discipline.
  • Same tasks, so the scores compare. Focus the session on the steps that still score badly.
  • This is the first round where the object should look like a product, so it is the first round where desirability can be tested. Plan your finish: colours, materials, icons applied.

Exercise 4: Build (rest of class)

Work on the refinements and the finish. Desk crits run during this time.
