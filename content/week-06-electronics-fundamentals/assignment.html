Part 1: Component Anatomy Map

Before you wire anything for your project, map what you have. For every part your hero experience needs, fill in one row:

Part Type Input or output? Voltage Arduino pins needed How it connects Solder? Where and why
Rotary encoder Module (PCB) Input 5V 2 digital + 1 for the push Loose header pins Yes: headers onto the PCB now. Later, stranded wires to it, because it sits in the lid.

Type is one of: board (the Arduino), module (a small PCB with a part on it), sensor, actuator / output (motor, LED, buzzer, display), power, or cable / connector.

Then draw a simple block diagram, by hand or digitally: the Arduino in the middle, each part around it, a line for each connection. Mark each line B (breadboard for now) or S (will be soldered).

Check your pin count against the UNO R4: 14 digital pins and 6 analog inputs. If you need more than that, flag it now.

Part 2: Claim a Part for the Class Library

The class library at ACCD-Fritzing-Parts has working examples for: LED, built-in LED, LED matrix, 8x8 LED matrix, 12-LED ring, push button, potentiometer. Over the next three weeks you add one more.

  1. Pick a component that is not in the library yet. Choose one from your BOJACK kit or your project parts, so there is no shipping wait. One you need for your project is the best choice.
  2. Claim it in the Slack thread for this assignment. First come, first served; no duplicates.
  3. Find its datasheet or product page, and note its voltage and pins.

Week 7: you get it working on your Arduino. Week 8: you document it and add it to the library. The full steps are in the library's CONTRIBUTE.md.
