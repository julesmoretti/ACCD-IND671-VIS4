Part 1: Finish the Class Circuit and Document It in Fritzing

Finish taking the class circuit through the whole path: breadboard, test, solder, test again, document.

  1. Finish the soldered board if you did not finish in class. Soldering happens in the shop, not at home: book a station during the week. Safety glasses on, fume extractor on. Check with continuity before you connect power.
  2. Test it: plug the red lead into the + rail and the black lead into the − rail of your powered breadboard. Module on: red LED on. Press the button: green LED on.
  3. Document it in Fritzing (reading, section 8; Fritzing is a paid download, about $12): the Breadboard view (breadboard, power source, resistors, LEDs, button, wires, same colours as your real circuit), a tidy Schematic view, the .fzz file and a .jpg export.

Part 2: Claim a Part for the Class Library

The class library at ACCD-Fritzing-Parts has working examples for: LED, built-in LED, LED matrix, 8x8 LED matrix, 12-LED ring, push button, potentiometer. Over the next two weeks you add one more, taking it through the same path as Part 1.

  1. Pick a component that is not in the library yet. Choose one from your BOJACK kit or your project parts, so there is no shipping wait. One you need for your project is the best choice.
  2. Claim it in the Slack thread for this assignment. First come, first served; no duplicates.
  3. Find its datasheet or product page, and note its voltage and pins.

Week 7: you get it working on your Arduino, on the breadboard. Week 8: you solder it onto a solderable breadboard, document it in Fritzing, and add it to the library. The full steps are in the library's CONTRIBUTE.md.
