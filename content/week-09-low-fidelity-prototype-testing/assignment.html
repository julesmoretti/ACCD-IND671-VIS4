Part 1: Finish the Low-Fidelity Prototype

Every Must-have task in your hero experience works end to end, inside a rough body that someone else can hold. See the Week 9 reading for what it has to do.

Part 2: Run Test Round 1

Follow the User Testing Rubric & Protocol, Round 1:

  • First, run and time your expert dry run of every step yourself, in a "UT1 Dry Run" tab.
  • 4–5 participants, classmates, including one naive participant.
  • 3–5 tasks covering every Must-have in your hero experience, broken into steps. Broad, not deep.
  • The UT1 table, one row per participant and the steps across, with score, time, emotion (with a verbatim quote in the note) and severity for every step. It goes in a new "UT1" tab of your HMI document.
  • Record the sessions if your participants agree (phone on a stand is fine).

Then write your round 1 baseline: the mean score per task, and the three lowest-scoring steps with what you plan to change.

Part 3: Start Your Enclosure

  • Measure every part and record the dimensions.
  • Block out the enclosure in CAD, placing inputs and outputs where round 1 suggests they should be.
  • Make an ergonomic check in foam core or cardboard: a rough mock-up, at true size, of the part the user holds, wears or presses (the grip, the main control panel, the part that sits on the body). Check it in the hand or on the body: reach, grip, and where the controls fall. Keep it rough; no 3D printing this week.
