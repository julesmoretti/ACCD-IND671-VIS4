Bring your UT1 tab, your session recordings, your enclosure CAD and your ergonomic check.

Exercise 1: Round 1 Gate Review (about 70 min)

This is not about a perfect model. It is about what did not work. Flaws are what we are looking for: the more honestly you show where your prototype failed, the more useful round 2 becomes. Be open about it, and be generous with each other.

One at a time, to the whole class, about 10 minutes each: 5 minutes presenting, 5 minutes of questions. Walk us through:

  1. What you tested: your tasks, your participants and your naive participant.
  2. Your recordings: show the moments that matter, not the whole session.
  3. What worked, with the scores behind it.
  4. Your three lowest-scoring steps, with what you saw and the participant's words behind each.
  5. What you will change for round 2, and where it shows up on the printed prototype.

Everyone else is the leadership team at a product gate review. Stress test every presenter: is the change aimed at the cause or the symptom? Is the evidence strong enough to justify it? What was not tested?

Exercise 2: Improve in Pairs (20 min)

In pairs, 10 minutes, then swap partners and go again. Using what came out of the gate review, and grounded in the evidence (the scores, what you saw, what participants said):

  • What to improve, and how: brainstorm fixes together, push back on each other's, and look for what would close the gap on the hero experience.
  • How to test it: is there a better way to test what failed? Which tasks get a quick re-test, which steps get finer sub-steps (scored, but left out of the task mean), and why.
  • Who and when: your round 2 participants (GradID students from other terms, no one who knows your project), and when you will test over the break.

Break (10 min)

Exercise 3: Build (65 min)

  • Form: update your CAD from round 1 and your ergonomic check. The printed body must enclose every button, light and board properly, and the parts the user holds, wears or presses must match your sketch. Start the print for the panel with the controls.
  • Circuit: move it off the full breadboard, one part at a time, testing after each move.
  • Missing parts: if any component needed to run the hero experience end to end is still missing, tell me today. At this point it is critical.
  • Labels: no colour or finish yet, but print your logo, icons and text on paper and glue them onto the print, so they read crisply.
  • Decals: start now. Lay out the decals your final model needs in Figma at true scale, and find out the lead time to get them made, so they are on the painted model in Week 12.
  • Desk crits run during this time: show me your CAD and your wiring plan.

Progress Check (15 min)

Each person, about 2 minutes: what you got done today, and the gap you will close before round 2.
