Bring your UT1 tab, your session recordings, your enclosure CAD and your ergonomic check.

Exercise 1: Round 1 Gate Review (about 70 min)

One at a time, to the whole class, about 10 minutes each: 5 minutes presenting, 5 minutes of questions. Walk us through:

  1. What you tested: your tasks, your participants and your naive participant.
  2. Your recordings: show the moments that matter, not the whole session.
  3. What worked, with the scores behind it.
  4. Your three lowest-scoring steps, with what you saw and the participant's words behind each.
  5. What you will change for round 2, and where it shows up on the printed prototype.

Everyone else is the leadership team at a product gate review. Stress test every presenter: is the change aimed at the cause or the symptom? Is the evidence strong enough to justify it? What was not tested?

Exercise 2: Improve in Pairs (20 min)

In pairs, 10 minutes, then swap partners and go again. Using what came out of the gate review:

  • What to improve, and how. Push back on each other's fixes.
  • Which tasks get a quick re-test, and which steps get finer sub-steps (scored, but left out of the task mean).
  • Who your round 2 participants are (GradID students from other terms), and when you will test over the break.

Break (10 min)

Exercise 3: Build (65 min)

  • Update your CAD from round 1 and your ergonomic check. The printed body must enclose every button, light and board properly, and the parts the user holds, wears or presses must match your sketch. Start the print for the panel with the controls.
  • Move your circuit off the full breadboard, one part at a time, testing after each move.
  • Labels: no colour or finish yet, but print your logo, icons and text on paper and glue them onto the print, so they read crisply.
  • Decals: start now. List the decals your final model needs and find out how long they take to get made, so they are ready to go on the painted model in round 3.
  • Desk crits run during this time: show me your CAD and your wiring plan.

Progress Check (15 min)

Each person, about 2 minutes: what you got done today, and the gap you will close before round 2.
