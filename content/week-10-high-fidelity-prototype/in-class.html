Bring your round 1 tables, your enclosure CAD and your ergonomic check.

Exercise 1: Round 1 Review (30 min)

In pairs, each person shares:

  1. Their three lowest-scoring steps, with the observation behind each.
  2. What the naive participant did with the device.
  3. The change they plan for each, and where it shows up in the 3D form.

The group's job is to push back: is the change aimed at the cause, or at the symptom?

Exercise 2: Plan Round 2 (15 min)

  • Which tasks get a quick re-test, and which get extra sub-steps (scored, but left out of the task mean)?
  • Who are your participants (GradID students from other terms), and when will you test over the break?

Break (10 min)

Exercise 3: Build (2 hours 5 min)

  • Update your CAD from round 1 findings and your ergonomic check. Start the print for the panel with the controls.
  • Move your circuit off the full breadboard, one part at a time, testing after each move.
  • Desk crits run during this time: show me your CAD and your wiring plan.
